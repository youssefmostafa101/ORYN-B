Are these products dietary supplements?

Yes. OUTPUT and DEEP STATE are presented as dietary supplements. Final product classification and labeling should match the finished manufacturer-approved label.

Where are the formulas made?

The current brand direction is USA-made. Production, quality controls, testing and final label details should be confirmed with the contracted manufacturer before launch.

Can I use OUTPUT every day?

The intended concept is a daily creatine routine. Follow the final serving instructions on the finished label and consult a qualified healthcare professional for questions about your individual use.

Can I use DEEP STATE every day?

The intended concept is a daily supplement routine. Follow the finished label's directions and consult a qualified healthcare professional for questions specific to you.

Why does ORYN B use outcome-led names?

The naming system is designed to make the role of each product obvious at a glance: OUTPUT for the training/performance formula and DEEP STATE for the evening-focused formula.

Is this medical advice?

No. ORYN B content is general product information and is not medical advice or a diagnosis.
